For my Week 2 portfolio in ITEP 414 - System Administration and Maintenance, I prepared an Enterprise Infrastructure Plan for NovaTech Startup Solutions, a fictional software company with 20 employees across IT, HR, Finance and Sales.

The project covers hardware, software and network inventories; a labeled network topology; system administration roles; and recommendations for security, backups and growth.

I practiced business requirements analysis, infrastructure planning, inventory preparation, technical documentation and network design. One important lesson was that equipment quantities, network connections and access policies must agree before deployment begins.

My design includes 14 desktops, six Sales laptops, a Linux server, backup storage, two managed switches and two wireless access points. VLANs separate departments, shared services and guest access.
